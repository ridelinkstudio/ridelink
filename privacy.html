RideLink Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

RideLink is committed to protecting the privacy of drivers, business owners, customers, and visitors who use our website and services. This policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected.

1. About RideLink

RideLink helps drivers and businesses establish an online presence through professional profile pages, promotional tools, and direct customer connection services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information voluntarily provided by users, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • WhatsApp contact information
  • Business or service details
  • Vehicle information
  • Photos and promotional content
  • Messages or enquiries submitted to RideLink

We may also collect basic website usage information such as:

  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Pages visited
  • Visit duration
  • General traffic statistics

3. How We Use Information

  • Create and maintain profile pages
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve RideLink services
  • Communicate important updates
  • Protect against misuse or fraud

4. Public Information

Information submitted for publication on RideLink pages may be publicly visible. This can include business names, vehicle details, service descriptions, contact numbers, WhatsApp links, and images.

5. Cookies & Analytics

RideLink may use cookies and analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with our website and to improve user experience.

6. Third-Party Services

RideLink pages may contain links to third-party services such as WhatsApp, Google Maps, social media platforms, payment services, or advertising networks. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

7. Advertising

RideLink may display advertisements through providers such as Google AdSense. These providers may use cookies and related technologies to personalize advertisements.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

RideLink is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

10. Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Updates will be posted on this page along with a revised date.
